Chicken Fettuccine Alfredo

Description

Chicken fettuccine alfredo is a very delicious dish! It requires very few ingredients and it is super easy to make. Whether you need to feed one person or ten, this dish can be scaled to your individual needs.

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast
  • Seasoning of your choice for the chicken
  • Fettuccine noodles
  • Alfredo sauce of your choice
  • Parsley

Steps

  1. Thaw chicken breast
  2. Season chicken
  3. Fill pot with water and heat water to a boil
  4. Add noodles to the water
  5. Cook noodles thoroughly and strain the water
  6. Add alfredo sauce to the strained noodles and mix it in
  7. Grill chicken
  8. Slice chicken and add to fettuccine alfredo
  9. Add diced parsley to meal and mix
  10. Enjoy!
