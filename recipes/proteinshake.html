Protein Shake

Description

Protein shakes are an amazing way to get protein in fast. You can make them however thick or thin you want so you can drink them fast or slow.

Ingredients

  • Protein powder
  • Your favorite fruits
  • Milk or water
  • Ice

Steps

  1. Scoop protien powder into a blender
  2. Add your favorite fruits
  3. Add one cup of ice
  4. Add 6 ounces of your desired liquid
  5. Blend and enjoy!
