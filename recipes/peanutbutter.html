Peanut Butter and Jelly Sandwich

Description

Peanut butter and jelly sandwiches are one of the best meals or snacks to make in a pinch. They are quite easy to make and are very filling. Whether you want a late night snack or need to pack a quick lunch, pb&j is the way to go!

Ingredients

  • Peanut butter of your choice
  • Jelly of your choice
  • Bread of your choice

Steps

  1. Grab a butter knife
  2. Take 2 pieces of bread and lay them on a plate
  3. Spread peanut butter on one piece of bread
  4. Clean the knife
  5. Spread jelly on the other piece of bread
  6. Put the 2 pieces of bread together
  7. Enjoy!
